What are Effort Values (EVs)?

Effort Values (EVs) are hidden training points that improve a Pokémon’s stats. Every Pokémon you defeat or item you use can contribute EVs to specific stats, shaping how your Pokémon performs in battle.

All EV Stat Categories

  • HP: Increases overall durability and survivability
  • Attack: Improves physical move damage
  • Defense: Reduces damage from physical attacks
  • Special Attack: Increases special move power
  • Special Defense: Reduces damage from special attacks
  • Speed: Determines turn order in battle

How EV Investment Works

Each Pokémon can earn a total of 510 EV points, with a maximum of 252 EVs per stat. Every 4 EVs equals roughly +1 point in that stat.

This means you must choose carefully where to invest your EVs depending on your strategy.

Example Strategy:

A fast attacker might use:

  • 252 Speed EVs
  • 252 Attack EVs
  • 4 HP EVs

Why EVs Matter

EV training allows two identical Pokémon to perform completely differently in battle. A properly trained Pokémon can outspeed opponents, survive hits, or deal significantly more damage.

Competitive players use EVs to specialize Pokémon roles such as sweepers, tanks, or support units.
