All EV Stat Categories

  • HP: Hit Point (HP) EVs increase the Pokémon's maximum HP. This leads to increased survivability in battle only when it comes to taking damage.
  • Attack: Attack EVs improve the power of physical moves.
  • Defense: Defense EVs reduce damage from physical attacks which help the Pokémon survive physical attacks, and can lead to increased durability when paired with HP EVs.
  • Special Attack: Special attack EVs increase the power of special moves.
  • Special Defense: Special defense EVs reduce damage from special attacks which help the Pokémon survive special attacks, and can lead to increased durability when paired with HP EVs.
  • Speed: Speed EVs increase the Pokémon's speed, which when going against another Pokémon if your speed stat is higher, allows you to move first in battle most of the time.

How EV Investment Works

Each Pokémon can earn a total of 510 EV points, with a maximum of 252 EVs per stat. Every 8 EVs equals roughly +1 point in that stat at level 50, whereas every 4 EVs equals roughly +1 point at level 100. Due to the nature of EVs being rounded down however, at Level 50, the first 4 EVs in a stat will give a +1 to the stat.

Due to the limited amount of EVs available, you must choose carefully where to invest your EVs depending on your strategy for a given Pokémon. For example, a physical attacker might focus on Attack and Speed EVs allowing it to deal more physical damage and potentially out speed opponents, while a defensive Pokémon might prioritize HP and Defense or Special Defense EVs allowing it to survive attacks more effectively.

Example Strategies:

A fast attacker might use:

  • 4 HP EVs
  • 252 Attack/Special Attack EVs
  • 252 Speed EVs

A bulky tank might use:

  • 252 HP EVs
  • 252 Defense/Special Defense EVs
  • 4 Speed EVs

Why EVs Matter

EV training allows two identical Pokémon to perform completely differently in battle. A properly trained Pokémon can outspeed opponents, survive hits, or deal significantly more damage.

Competitive players use EVs to specialize Pokémon roles such as sweepers, tanks, or support units. They often time choose to EV train Pokémon to counteract specific threats or playstyles in order to be able to take be able to have ways of dealing with unfavorable matchups, or to improve their strengths.
