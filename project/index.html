EV Training and It's Purpose

EV training is the primary way of increasing Pokemon's stats to increase their effectiveness in a battle. Through the use of EVs, you can tailor a Pokémon's stats to fit a specific role or strategy. This allows for Pokémon to have different playstyles and means that almost every Pokémon will be unique to the player who trained it. Player who play competitively will EV train to either maximize their Pokémon's capabilities, or have to face against certain opponents they are likely to encounter, or are difficult for that Pokémon to face.

What Are EVs?

Effort Values (EVs) are hidden training points that improve a Pokémon’s stats. They are increased by defeating Pokémon in battle that have a high stat in the EV you are wanting to increase. YOu can increase EVs this way further by having your Pokémon hold "Power" items. You can also increase EVs by using Vitamins, which increase EVs by 10 points each. If you have trained EVs incorrectly, or you want to reduce them, you can use berries that will decrease a specified EV.

How They Change the Game

When Pokémon are EV trained, they can survive hits that would otherwise knock them out, deal more damage, outspeed opponents, or be able to handle specific threats to a competitive team. Learning how to EV train efficiently will allow trainers to not only gain an understanding of a mechanic of the game, but also be able to build competitive teams.

Other Factors

In addition to EVs, there are other factors that can influence how powerful a Pokémon is. Abilities provide a 10% increase to a Pokémon's stats while also providing a 10% decrease to another stat, which can allow stats that are not useful to specific Pokémon to be decreased while increasing a stat that is useful to a Pokémon. There are also held items that can increase a Pokémon's damage, a Pokémon's moveset which can be tailored to take advantage of their EVs, and a Pokémon's base stats which help trainers know what EV stats to invest in, as well as know if the Pokémon will fit the role they need.
